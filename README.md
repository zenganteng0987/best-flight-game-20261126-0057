Skylane Ridge — a valley flight game. Fly the rings over the hills, mind your throttle and your fuel, and bring her home to runway 36. Three sorties (Ring Run, Ridge Tour, Valley Landing), chase and cockpit views, a lit instrument panel, and synthesised engine and wind sound. All art and sound are drawn and synthesised in code; the game runs offline from static files.
